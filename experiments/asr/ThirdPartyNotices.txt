Historical experimental ASR components; not bundled with the app.

Final speech recognition can run a separate local MLX worker.
Qwen runtime: MLX Audio 0.6.2 (MIT): https://github.com/Blaizzy/mlx-audio
Cohere runtime: MLX Speech 0.5.4 (MIT): https://github.com/AppAutomaton/mlx-speech
MLX 0.32.4: https://github.com/ml-explore/mlx (MIT).
The selected model and exact revision are recorded in ASRSelection.json.
Qwen3-ASR: https://github.com/QwenLM/Qwen3-ASR (Apache License 2.0).
Cohere Transcribe: https://huggingface.co/CohereLabs/cohere-transcribe-03-2026
(license metadata: Apache License 2.0).
Cohere MLX conversion: https://huggingface.co/appautomaton/cohere-asr-mlx
(license metadata: Apache License 2.0).
Worker dependencies and model weights are installed separately by the local setup
script. Installed Python packages retain their own license metadata. The worker
sets offline mode; it does not upload recordings to a transcription API.
